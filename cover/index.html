What we built

We used Codex to build a chat for Madison Plume, a graphic novelist who explains civics, economics, and media literacy. The local AI demo runs SmolLM2 135M or 360M through Transformers.js. For covered questions, it gives the model a checked notebook entry and shows that entry alongside the generated reply.

Why we changed course

The small models sometimes produced garbled text, incomplete replies, or misleading explanations. We checked the notebook against public sources, including the National Archives and the Federal Reserve. The newest version serves 21 edited answers directly, with Madison’s humor and links to the sources.

From the project conversation

What we concluded

Running locally keeps the conversation on the device. A checked note in the prompt still left room for misleading replies. The edited notebook gave us control over accuracy and voice. Both chats can continue offline in the same tab after loading online. For the AI demo, wait for the model to finish downloading and show Ready.
